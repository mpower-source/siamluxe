Critical Risks

Single-supplier dependencyOne supplier collapse = no product line. Mitigation: identify backup supplier at Platinum by Drop 03.
No logistics infrastructure yetCannot publicly sell without a shipping solution. Mitigation: Kerry Express (domestic) + DHL test shipment before Drop 01 goes public.
Zero social proof at launchLuxury positioning with no reviews. Mitigation: first 10 buyers treated as VIPs — personal notes, photo requests, early access rewards.
Trademark not filedBrand at risk before scaling. Mitigation: file USPTO before public launch at scale. ~$300.

Structural Risks

DM model doesn't scale50–100 DMs/day is a full-time job. Mitigation: build email list from Day 1 — every buyer goes on the list.
No owned customer dataInstagram + LINE are rented audiences. Mitigation: Klaviyo email list capturing from bio link immediately.
Model is easily replicatedAnyone can walk into Platinum. Mitigation: brand, story, and relationships are the moat — not the product.
Quiet luxury trend peakingEntering a maturing trend. Mitigation: Thai provenance + DM relationship is the differentiation. Not just the aesthetic.
